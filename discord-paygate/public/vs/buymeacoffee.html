Frequently asked

How much does Buy Me a Coffee cost compared to Dues?

Buy Me a Coffee's publicly listed pricing is 5% of earnings. Dues charges a flat plan (free up to 10 paying members, then from $14.99/mo) and takes 0% of your sales.

Does Dues really take 0% of sales?

Yes. Dues charges a flat monthly plan only. Stripe’s standard card-processing fees still apply, as they do on every platform.

Where does my money go with Dues?

Straight into your own Stripe account. Dues never holds, routes, or freezes your funds.

Can I switch without losing my members?

Your members keep their Discord roles while you set Dues up, and your Stripe customers stay in your own Stripe account either way.

Switching from Buy Me a Coffee?

Free for your first 10 paying members — no card required.
