Frequently asked

How much does Patreon cost compared to Ripley?

Patreon's publicly listed pricing is 8–12% of earnings. Ripley charges a flat plan (free up to 10 paying members, then from $9.99/mo) and takes 0% of your sales.

Does Ripley really take 0% of sales?

Yes. Ripley charges a flat monthly plan only. Stripe’s standard card-processing fees still apply, as they do on every platform.

Where does my money go with Ripley?

Straight into your own Stripe account. Ripley never holds, routes, or freezes your funds.

Can I switch without losing my members?

Your members keep their Discord roles while you set Ripley up, and your Stripe customers stay in your own Stripe account either way.

Switching from Patreon?

Free for your first 10 paying members — no card required.
