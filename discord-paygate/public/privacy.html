Privacy

What we store

When you sign in with Discord we store your Discord ID, username, and an access token used solely to add you to the community with your purchased role. When you buy, we store your purchase (plan, status, dates) so your access can be managed. Optional order notes you write at checkout are visible to the server owner.

What we never see

Your card details go directly to Stripe on their hosted checkout page — they never touch this site.

Cookies

Only functional cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in, and short-lived cookies that make Discord sign-in work. No tracking, no ads.

Removal

Want your data gone? Contact the server owner on Discord and your account records will be deleted (purchase records may be retained where required for accounting).
