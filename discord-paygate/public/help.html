Everything Dues does, in about two minutes. Every card links to the real thing.

Set up in four steps

1. Open the dashboard and sign in with Discord.

2. Pick your server and invite the Dues bot.

3. Connect Stripe with an API key — payments go straight to your Stripe account.

4. Create a product, attach the role it unlocks, publish. Your store is live at dues.gg/your-store.

Frequently asked

My Discord role went missing — what do I do?

Open your account page and hit "Re-sync my access". It re-checks your purchases and puts the role back. If it still fails, message the server owner on Discord.

How do I cancel a membership?

From your account page, any time — no asking anyone. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Where does the money go?

Directly to the seller’s own Stripe account on every sale. Dues never sits between you and your payout — Stripe pays out on its normal schedule.

Do I need the Stripe dashboard to run my store?

No. Connect Stripe once with an API key; after that products, prices, discounts and refund-worthy situations are all handled from the Dues dashboard.
