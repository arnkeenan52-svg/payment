Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be in the Discord server before buying?

No. If you're not in the server yet, you're added automatically the moment your payment completes — with your role already attached.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. Payment happens on Stripe's own hosted checkout page. This site never sees, stores, or transmits your card details.

What does "lifetime" actually mean?

One payment, permanent access. There is no subscription behind it and nothing that renews or expires.

My role disappeared — what do I do?

Open your account page and press "Re-sync my access". Your roles are restored instantly. An automatic hourly check does the same in the background.

Can I get a refund?

Access is delivered instantly, so completed purchases are generally final — but if something went wrong (double charge, nothing delivered), contact the owner on Discord and it will be sorted. Details in the terms.
