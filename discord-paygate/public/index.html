Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid?

Directly. Buyers pay on Stripe checkout connected to your own Stripe account — money never passes through Ripley, and there is no fee on sales.

How much does Ripley cost?

Free for your first 10 paying members. Past that it’s a flat monthly plan — $5.99 up to 50 members, $19.99 up to 500, $49.99 unlimited. Never a percentage of your sales.

Do buyers have to be in my server before purchasing?

No. If they’re not in the server yet, they’re added automatically the moment payment completes — with the role already attached.

Can I sell one-time lifetime access?

Yes — one-time lifetime and monthly subscriptions are both supported. Lifetime access structurally cannot expire.

Can I remove a member or add one manually?

Yes. The dashboard’s Members tab lets you revoke a membership (the role comes off immediately), grant access by hand, or re-sync anyone.

Is card data safe?

Card details go straight to Stripe on their hosted checkout page. This site never sees, stores, or transmits them.

A buyer’s role disappeared — now what?

They press "Re-sync my access" on their account page and it’s restored instantly. An hourly sweep does the same automatically.
