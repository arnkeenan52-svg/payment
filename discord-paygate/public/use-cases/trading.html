Frequently asked

Do I need my own Stripe account?

Yes — that is the point. Every payment lands directly in your own Stripe account. Dues never holds your money.

What happens when a member cancels?

When the subscription ends, Dues removes the paid role automatically. Members in good standing are re-checked hourly.

Can I charge different prices for different channels?

Yes. Each product maps to its own Discord role, so you can sell tiered access at different prices.
