[title]
How Dues Works

[subtitle]
Four steps to selling. Two minutes.

[intro]
Dues turns a Discord server into a store. You sell roles, buyers pay by card,
and the role lands automatically. Payments go to your own Stripe account —
Dues never holds your money and takes 0% of it.

**There are no slash commands.** Everything runs from the dashboard on the web.
Nothing to memorise, nothing to type in chat — the bot only needs to be in your
server so it can hand out the roles you sell.

[steps]
Invite — add Dues to your server and pick a name for your store.
Connect — paste your Stripe key, set a price, pick the role it unlocks.
Access — members pay on your store page and their role lands in seconds.
Payouts — money settles in your own Stripe account. Dues never holds it.

[links]
Start your store | https://dues.gg/dashboard
Read the docs | https://dues.gg/help

[outro]
**Buying rather than selling?** Pay on the seller's store page and your role is
applied to your Discord account within seconds. Your receipt arrives by email
and you can manage or cancel anytime from your account page.

**Stuck?** Open a ticket in {support} with your order email or order id. Read
{rules} first — rule 6 in particular, because staff never DM you first.
