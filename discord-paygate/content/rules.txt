[title]
Server Rules

[subtitle]
Read once. They are short.

[intro]
Dues is a payment platform, so this server has people's money in it. Most of
these rules exist to keep that from going wrong. Breaking one gets you a
warning; breaking 5, 6, 7 or 8 does not.

[rules]
Be civil. No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks.
No spam, mass mentions, or unsolicited DMs.
No advertising or recruiting for other servers, stores, or services.
Support goes in {support}. Include your order email or order id.
Never post card numbers, bot tokens, API keys, or passwords. Not even in a ticket.
Staff never DM you first. Anyone who DMs you asking for payment, tokens, or passwords is a scammer. Report it in {support}.
No impersonating Dues staff, sellers, or other members.
Do not leak or resell content you bought through a Dues store.
Open a ticket in {support} before you go to your bank. A chargeback is not a refund route - it costs the seller a fee on top of the money and takes weeks to resolve. Asking us is faster.
Sellers: deliver what your listing promises, and honour your own refund policy.
Follow Discord's Terms of Service. Nothing illegal, no NSFW.
Staff decisions are final. Raise disputes in {support}, not in public.

[outro]
Official links are in {official-links} and nowhere else. If you found a "Dues"
link somewhere else, it is not ours.
